# Why Offers OSS matters in today's IT job market

**1. The volume arms race backfired.** AI auto-apply bots made it possible to fire off thousands of applications in days; reporters and developers have described bots applying to 1,000+ jobs in two days ([Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/12/2024/linkedins-have-nots-and-have-bots)). Recruiters answered with stricter ATS filters and knockout questions, and platforms restrict accounts that automate. Spray-and-pray now costs you accounts and replies. Offers OSS bets on **precision**: fewer, better-fitted, human-submitted applications.

**2. ATS screening is keyword-first.** Most mid and large IT employers filter with an ATS before a human reads anything. Offers OSS's local fit score shows the matched and missing keywords per job *before* you spend time, and the right resume variant is picked automatically.

**3. Every IT profile now has many shapes.** A Java developer can be pitched as backend, full-stack, integration/support or AI-enabled engineer. Offers OSS routes each JD to the right resume variant, so one person can run 3-4 targeted pipelines without chaos.

**4. AI skills are table stakes - and so is judgement about AI.** Hiring managers increasingly look for engineers who use AI well. A public, compliant, token-efficient AI workflow on your GitHub is itself a portfolio piece: it shows agent design, prompt engineering, data hygiene and respect for platform rules.

**5. Follow-ups win offers.** Most candidates never follow up. Offers OSS schedules it (Applied +7 days, Interview +2) and drafts the message.

**6. Layoffs and fast switches.** When you need a new role in weeks, not months, you need a daily system with a dashboard, not a messy spreadsheet. Offers OSS was built during exactly that situation.

## Who it helps
- Freshers and career-switchers: profile + answers bank removes blank-page panic.
- Experienced engineers: multi-variant routing, follow-up discipline, interview learning log.
- Career-gap returners: honest gap wording stored once (`brain/answers.yaml` A04) and reused consistently.
- Non-IT roles: the skill bank is just a list; replace it with your field's keywords.
